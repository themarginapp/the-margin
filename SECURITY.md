# Security

If you have found a security problem in The Margin, please email [hello@themarginapp.com](mailto:hello@themarginapp.com) with "Security" in the subject, or use GitHub's private "Report a vulnerability" button on this repository. Please do not open a public issue.

Tell us what you found, how to reproduce it and what an attacker could do with it. We will confirm we received it within three working days and keep you posted until it is fixed.

Please test only against your own account or the no-account sandbox at `https://mcp.themarginapp.com/sandbox/mcp`. Do not access other people's data, and do not run anything that degrades the service for others.
